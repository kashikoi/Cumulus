Welcome aboard

Your whole financial sky, in one view.

Cumulus keeps every account, bill, and paycheck in one calm place — no logins, no bank connections, no spreadsheets. Everything lives right here in your browser, down to a slider that shows exactly when a debt disappears and what it’ll cost you to get there. Here’s the whole thing in about three minutes.

  1. 1

    Add your accounts

    Hit + Add account and drop in anything you own or owe: checking, savings, 401k, your house, credit cards, car loans, the internet bill. Give it a name, a balance, and optionally the website link so it gets the real logo.

    Own crypto? Pick the Cryptocurrency type, look up a coin by symbol or name, and enter how much you hold. Cumulus fetches the coin’s icon and prices your holding in real dollars from there — no manual balance to maintain.

    🏦 Checking 💎 Savings 📈 Investments 🪙 Crypto 🏡 Home 💳 Credit cards 🎓 Student loan 🌐 Internet
  2. 2

    Everything sorts itself

    Accounts drop into tidy categories automatically — Income, Cash & Savings, Investments, Property, Credit Cards, Loans, and Monthly Bills. Each category shows its own running total, and your net worth updates at the top instantly. For a home, that math is home value minus what’s left on the mortgage — your real equity, not just a balance.

    Nice detail: paychecks and monthly bills are treated as cash flow, so they never inflate or dent your net worth.

  3. 3

    Rearrange it like an iPhone

    Press and hold any card until everything starts to wiggle, then drag cards into whatever order you like. Grab the empty space inside a category to drag the whole category around. Click anywhere outside to finish.

  4. 4

    Update balances from a screenshot

    The neatest trick in the app. Screenshot your balance from your bank’s site, click the ⟳ button on the card, and press ⌘V. Cumulus reads the number right out of the image and fills it in.

    Logo not loading? Screenshot it, then use Paste screenshot in the account’s Icon field to set your own. Crypto accounts skip this step entirely — their price refreshes on its own, and there’s a ⟳ on the card any time you want it sooner.

  5. 5

    Your paychecks, on schedule

    Add a Paycheck / Salary account with your take-home amount, whether you’re paid every two weeks or twice a month, and the date of your last check. Cumulus works out every future payday from there — no manual math, and it’s what powers the purple paydays you’ll see later on the calendar.

  6. 6

    Track what’s due — and what you paid

    Give any bill, card, or loan a due day and a min / expected amount and it lands in the Cash Flow section below your accounts, split into Past due and Upcoming activity so nothing slips by unnoticed. Upcoming activity itself is two side-by-side lists: still-open items on the left, and everything you’ve already handled on the right. Tap Mark paid and it moves to Completed instantly, no confirmation needed — the amount defaults to what you set on the account but you can tweak it first if this time’s payment is different. Changed your mind? Tap ↩ Return to activity on the Completed side to send it right back.

    Check the box on one Cash & Savings account (in the Accounts list above) and Upcoming activity will track its real balance — showing $start ($estimate) for each paycheck, and auto-deducting as you mark bills paid.

    Waiting on a check or a transfer that hasn’t hit your account yet? Hit + Pending — it shows up at the top of Activity, adjusts your checked account’s balance until you tap Mark received (or Mark sent, if you’re the one paying), then moves to Completed just like a bill.

    Completed only shows this month and next — everything you’ve ever marked paid or received stays saved though, and 🕑 History opens a scrollable popup with all of it.

  7. 7

    Watch a debt count down to zero

    Add an APR to any credit card, loan, or mortgage and its card grows a small progress bar and a payoff date — no extra setup beyond the rate you already know.

    Paid off Apr 2028 · 62% paid off View payoff ›

    Tap View payoff to open the full explorer: drag the payment slider and watch the payoff date, total interest, and a principal-vs-interest bar all react instantly — plus exactly how many months and dollars you’d save by paying more aggressively. There’s a full month-by-month schedule underneath if you want the receipts.

    No APR entered yet? The teaser simply stays hidden on that card — nothing half-finished to look at.

  8. 8

    See the rest of your year

    The projection table runs from today all the way through January of next year, month by month: money in, money out, and what your balance should look like when the dust settles — it already knows about every payday and every bill you’ve set up, so there’s nothing extra to configure.

  9. 9

    A calendar that speaks in color

    Widen your browser window and a calendar and calculator float in beside the app.

    15 Red — something’s due
    15 Green — payday!
    15 Blue — today
    ⏰ Clock — a payoff-by date you set

    Only the most recent past payday plus today’s and upcoming ones stay lit — older paydays fade back to normal.

    Hover any highlighted day for a quick popup listing exactly what’s due, paid, or landing that day.

  10. 10

    Settings, your data, your rules

    The ⚙️ button in the header holds dark mode, this tutorial, and everything about your data. Export saves a full backup file, Import brings one back, Randomize demo data fills the app with a realistic sample in one click, and Clear all data wipes the slate clean.

    Flip on Account review checklist and your checking, savings, and credit card cards get a quick 🔍 to confirm you’ve looked over recent activity — it un-checks itself again after your next payday.

One important thing

Everything you enter is stored only in this browser on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, which is great for privacy — but it also means clearing your browser data would clear your accounts, and the same data won’t appear in a different browser. Export a backup from Settings now and then to be safe.

That’s the whole app.

Go add an account and watch your sky fill in.
